# Public content and disclosure boundary

## Governing rule

Publish only information Hydar can personally substantiate and is comfortable defending in an interview. When a professional detail is uncertain, sensitive, confidential, or not independently approved, omit it from the public site and CV.

## Professional projects

SAFAPAC and AIRIS may name AMIC, the project, and the public Airbus collaboration. Public content may include a high-level purpose, Hydar's role, technologies personally used, generalized challenges, personal engineering decisions and trade-offs, verified non-sensitive results, role-based collaboration context, and diagrams newly created for the portfolio.

Never publish or reproduce:

- Confidential or proprietary reports
- Company-owned source code
- Internal screenshots or copied diagrams
- Proprietary formulas or business logic
- Credentials, tokens, internal URLs, or infrastructure identifiers
- Customer or stakeholder data
- Detailed live security configuration
- Confidential costs, budgets, or unapproved internal performance results

SAFAPAC must be described as a deployed beta/internal-validation platform. The narrative may explain that Hydar inherited an early boilerplate application and became primarily responsible for backend, database, and AWS operations, then substantially rebuilt and productionized that scope. It must not claim the entire product was built alone or entirely from scratch.

AIRIS is a supporting contribution. Public claims may cover workload/concurrency testing, bottleneck diagnosis, optimization research, engineering handoff, and stakeholder training. They must not claim ownership of the architecture, implementation of recommendations, or resulting production improvements.

## Personal and internship work

AnotherEdenAI is public and may use richer screenshots and implementation visuals, but it must remain labelled as actively developed until current evaluation and deployment gates are complete.

PETRONAS Digital work must be framed as collaborative MEAN/Ionic onboarding-prototype work, primarily involving Angular/Ionic frontend delivery, mobile deployment, troubleshooting, and documentation. Unsupported productivity metrics are prohibited.

## Personal contact

- The public website and public resume expose email, LinkedIn, and GitHub, but not the phone number.
- The ignored private application CV includes the phone number for recruiter calls and WhatsApp.
- Phone exposure on the public site may be enabled later only through an explicit privacy-policy decision.

## Repository boundary

Private evidence remains local, ignored, and untracked. Public MDX, diagrams, screenshots, and resume artifacts require a manual disclosure review before staging. Ignore rules and automated path checks reduce accidental exposure but do not replace human review.
